# Professional Self-Assessment
Completing the Computer Science program and building my ePortfolio has allowed me to showcase my technical strengths, reflect on my growth, and prepare for my professional goals in data analysis and systems engineering. Over the course of the program, I gained practical experience in full-stack development, data analytics, and software design, applying these skills through a combination of academic projects and self-driven enhancements. The capstone process in particular demonstrated my ability to design, test, and improve professional-quality solutions while maintaining a focus on performance, usability, and security.

Developing the ePortfolio gave me a structured way to demonstrate not only what I built but also how I approached each challenge. Each artifact reflects a key stage of my professional development: designing efficient algorithms, creating scalable database structures, and applying secure coding practices. For example, in my CS-340 dashboard project, I implemented data-driven dashboards using Dash and MongoDB, enhanced through structured testing, caching, and schema validation. These enhancements display both technical competence and a problem-solving mindset focused on reliability and performance, qualities that I will continue to bring into my professional career.

Throughout my coursework, I learned the importance of collaboration and communication in achieving technical goals. As a former U.S. Navy Operations Specialist and watch supervisor, I was already accustomed to coordinating among teams under pressure. The program further developed that experience by requiring structured teamwork in shared development environments, version control, and peer review. I also learned to communicate with stakeholders through written and visual documentation, skills refined in project design documents, software requirement analyses, and code review scripts. These abilities ensure I can translate technical processes into actionable insights for both technical and non-technical audiences.

My coursework strengthened my technical foundation in data structures and algorithms, emphasizing efficiency and scalability. I learned to transform raw data into structured insights, optimizing performance with vectorized operations and defensive programming. In software engineering and databases, I developed strong design habits by separating logic, layout, and data access layers, and by applying reusable modules and consistent documentation practices. Finally, my understanding of security evolved through every stage of development, from anticipating vulnerabilities in software architecture to implementing data validation, encryption, and error handling to protect data integrity and privacy.

Together, the artifacts in my portfolio represent a complete view of my growth as a computer scientist. The dashboard application demonstrates my ability to design and optimize complex data systems. The algorithmic enhancements highlight my problem-solving and analytical strengths. The database improvements show my commitment to secure, resilient system design. Collectively, they illustrate my readiness to apply computer science principles in professional environments where reliability, innovation, and ethical responsibility are paramount.
